The LUMIÈRE story

From a 12-seat supper club above a bakery to one of the most-talked-about dining destinations in the city.

Our Philosophy

Dining as cinema.

LUMIÈRE was born from a simple obsession: what if a meal could feel like a film? Every course staged, every light placed with intention, every guest treated as the lead character.

We work with farmers, foragers, and fishers within 200 miles, then transform their work with techniques borrowed from Paris, Tokyo, and Copenhagen. The result: cuisine that is local in origin and limitless in imagination.

The Journey

A timeline of moments

2019

The first 12 seats

Chef Antoine opens a pop-up supper club above a bakery. Sold out in 4 hours.

A real address

LUMIÈRE moves to Aurora Lane. 40 seats. First write-up in the New York Times.

2023

Awarded a Michelin Guide entry and the James Beard nomination for Best New Restaurant.

Expanded to 80 seats with a chef's counter, open kitchen, and immersive lighting design.

Meet the Chef

A director at the pass.

Antoine trained under three-Michelin-star chefs in Paris and Kyoto before returning home to open LUMIÈRE. His cooking is precise, theatrical, and deeply personal — a love letter to the cities and producers that shaped him.

"I don't write menus. I write screenplays — and the plate is the screen."
